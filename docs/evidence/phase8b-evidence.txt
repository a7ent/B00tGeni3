HOW THE ART WAS MADE
  oTR-DaRkNeSs's original mockup.txt / LOGO1.TXT / ART folder are not on the build host (mdfind and find: no hits).
  The art is rebuilt from phase8/mockup/mockup-photo-2.png (a straight screen capture) by phase8/tools/gen-art.py:
    - cell grid of the capture: 4.667 x 9.333 px per character, origin x 333.2 y 137 (tools/grid.py, grid2.py)
    - OTR DARKNSS logo (98 x 11 cells) and B00TG3N13 logo (112 x 7 cells): one shade level 0-4 per cell,
      from the median brightness of each cell; drawn as solid blocks at 0/29/57/76/100 percent brightness
    - bottom banner: 864 x 48 px 1-bit bitmap, upsampled from the capture and thresholded
    - text: 132 items with row/column; the rebuilt 56-line text is phase8/art/mockup-rebuilt.txt
  Output: phase8/art/B00tArt.h, compiled into the .efi. All art is drawn with GOP Blt; the rest with ConOut.
  No 2025 B00tGeni3 code was used.

LAYOUTS (chosen in UiInit from ConOut columns x rows and the GOP resolution)
  full     console >= 124 x 56: the mockup's 56 lines, centred (1920x1080 = 240 x 67)
  compact  console >= 124 x 47: both logos at half size, 9 rows dropped, small star dropped (1024x768 = 128 x 48)
  neither, no GOP, or a console whose cells are not the builtin renderer's 8x16: the Phase 5 plain menu only
  UI scale 2 is handled in the code but NOT tested.

COLORS
  B00TG3N13 logo green; OTR DARKNSS logo and banner white; borders EFI_WHITE; labels EFI_LIGHTGRAY; URL EFI_LIGHTBLUE;
  boot-arg field black on light gray. The logo shades are brightness steps of white/green drawn by Blt, so they are
  outside the 16 text colors (needed for the shading the spec asks for). Yellow selection / red warnings come with 8c.

DIFFERENCES FROM THE MOCKUP (all approximations of a 1392x807 capture)
  - "By:" on line 27: the capture reads "Bu:" or "By:" at this size; "By:" used. NOT VERIFIED.
  - Skull U+2620 between "B00tGeni3" and "2025": the builtin font lacks it; replaced by an 8x16 bitmap drawn by CC.
  - Side border groups: "*#*" used throughout ("***" on line 6); the exact characters are not readable in the capture.
    Their column positions are taken from the capture.
  - Exact counts of '*' vs '#' at the ends of line 25 are a best reading.
  - Logo cells where the capture is between two shade levels were rounded to the nearer one.
  - Compact layout is CC's arrangement; the mockup has no small version.

8b SCOPE

  in code; NOT re-tested in 8b). Navigation, the info row values and the tools are 8c; boot-args are 8d.
  Fix history: build 1 drew logos after text (tagline and star hidden in full layout); build 2 fixed it; build 3
  hides the star in the compact layout. Earlier screenshots 20261115-1101xx/1106xx/1155xx/1156xx are from builds 1-2.
